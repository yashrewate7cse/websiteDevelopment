SUNNYSIDE COOP - egg farm website (v2, simple version)

KAISE CHALAYE
1. Zip ko unzip karo.
2. index.html par double-click karo. Browser me khul jayegi.
   Kuch install nahi karna. Internet ke bina bhi chalegi (sirf font alag dikhega).

FILES (sirf 3 kaam ki files)
- index.html : page ka content (text, drawings, sections)
- style.css  : rang, size, animation (sab upar se neeche numbered sections me)
- script.js  : interactions (cart, egg crack, scroll animation, farm tour)

APNA BANANE KE LIYE (easy)
1. Naam badlo: "Sunnyside Coop" ko Find & Replace se apne brand naam se badlo
   (index.html me).
2. Prices badlo: script.js ke sabse upar SETTINGS me.
3. Rang badlo: style.css ke sabse upar :root me.
4. Phone/email: index.html ke footer me.
5. Farm tour ka text: script.js ke SETTINGS me "tour".

ISSUES-AND-FIXES.md me saari problems aur unke solutions ki list hai.
